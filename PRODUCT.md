# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Recruiters, hiring managers, engineering leads, and potential collaborators evaluating Rashay Daya for junior DevOps, cloud, platform, and full-stack roles. They need to understand his technical range, verify shipped work, and reach his CV, GitHub, LinkedIn, or contact details quickly.

## Product Purpose

Present Rashay's production engineering work with enough concrete proof to establish credibility. Success means a visitor can identify his target roles, inspect representative systems and DevOps tooling, understand what he personally delivered, and take a hiring or collaboration action without hunting.

## Brand Personality

Technical, premium, and sharp. The portfolio should feel like a production engineering command center: systems-focused, controlled, specific, and credible.

## Anti-references

Generic developer portfolios, template-like card galleries, vague engineering manifestos, decorative terminal styling without technical substance, and unsupported metrics or proof.

## Design Principles

1. Show operational proof before making capability claims.
2. Make role, ownership, deployment state, and project access explicit.
3. Use the command-center language as a coherent system, not as decoration.
4. Keep primary hiring actions visible and unambiguous.
5. Separate Rashay's personal portfolio from Kaji Labs while making the relationship easy to understand.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Preserve full keyboard navigation, visible focus, semantic structure, sufficient contrast, readable mobile typography, and reduced-motion alternatives.
